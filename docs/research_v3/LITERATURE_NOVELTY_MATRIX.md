# V3 literature and novelty matrix

Reviewed 2026-11-04. This is a targeted overlap assessment, not an exhaustive priority search. Searches combined `dynamic electric vehicle routing`, `stochastic requests`, `capacitated charging`, `queue`, `ALNS`, `rollout`, and `commitment`, with emphasis on 2023-2026 work and references in the requested papers. `Full text` means an accessible article or manuscript was inspected; `abstract` means claims below are limited to a publisher, author-institution, or preprint abstract. An unreported feature is **unknown**, not proven absent. The V3 question is whether first actions from an ALNS fleet controller can be selected using sampled future demand and endogenous shared-charger contention while protecting accepted commitments under five seconds.

## Repository overlap and conclusion

The repository already has adaptive ALNS destroy/repair, partial-charge route reconstruction, a common five-second deadline, immutable dispatched physical actions, and counterfactual first-action screening. Its `committed_customers` tracks dispatched service, **not** business-level request acceptance; the historical plant does not simulate finite shared-charger queues. See [the prior research decision](../RESEARCH_DIRECTION_DECISION.md), [`evrp/optimizers/alns.py`](../../evrp/optimizers/alns.py), and [`evrp/targeted_recovery.py`](../../evrp/targeted_recovery.py).

The exact conjunction of *hard-window online EVRPTW, endogenous shared-charger queues, accepted promises, and a five-second ALNS first-action controller* was not verified in the reviewed sources. This absence is **not** a priority claim. All proposed V3 components have strong individual or adjacent precedent. The plan specifies their integration and evaluation, but no new mathematical principle, algorithmic operator, bound, or demonstrated causal failure that uniquely motivates it. The novelty gate therefore fails for an **algorithmic-method claim**. A narrower comparative question about when mean-wait approximations fail under endogenous queues remains possible after an independent problem-definition and benchmark audit.
