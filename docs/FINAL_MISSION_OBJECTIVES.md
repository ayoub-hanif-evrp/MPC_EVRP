## Corrections in finalization

  alongside the separately labeled equally weighted parent contrast.
- Unconditional operations are descriptions, not cross-method efficiency rankings.

## Selection and evidence boundaries

The frozen development rule states no aggregate service loss, no repeated
family harm, and useful service or conditional-quality improvement. The later
analyzer additionally required positive service, two improving parents, zero
harmed parents and nonnegative method-level changes. Those are different rules.
The stricter screen is disclosed as post hoc; one ACS loss alone does not prove
failure of the frozen wording. No candidate is retroactively promoted.

The cache supplement was frozen before its 15 new outcomes. New cache runs
pair with existing, hash-verified original results on indices 1/3/5. Together
with original component cases it contains 30 pairs on six parents. Dates differ,
so CPU ratios are shared-machine observations. MCTS and ACS can consume less
CPU yet travel farther on identical customer sets. This supports restricted
efficiency findings, not universal routing improvement.

The original five methods remain the selected independent publication result.
No change was tuned on primary or replication outcomes. Development adds no
independent parent and is not an enhanced holdout.

## Compute scope

The mission starts after reviewed commit `cff13c21`. Before the coverage
extension, replication, development, technical continuation and the probe
charged 14589.340302 seconds and recorded 13819.968750 worker CPU seconds over
185 attempts. The 15-job extension has a fixed additional 4200-second cap.
The completion audit sums actual ledgers, including failures, without charging
reused baselines twice, against eight charged hours and twelve worker CPU-hours.

Earlier performance-engineering, fast-framework and hybrid studies existed at
the reviewed commit. Their negative evidence is cited, not rerun. Charged
execution includes conservative setup allowances and is not exact calendar
time. Tests, replay and reporting are identified separately as validation work.
