# Assumptions and Limits

- The local Desaulniers 2016 format-1 and format-2 files are immutable
  benchmark inputs. The 2016 instances are static; customer releases are
  generated by this project's reference-preserving dynamicization.
- The active policy is multiple partial recharging (MP). A full charge is
  allowed; the published arc graph omits station-to-station travel.
- Vehicles are homogeneous, start full at the depot, have one depot-to-depot
  route, and cannot reload or reactivate after return. Stations have the
  published common hours and no modeled queue or concurrency limit.
- The common objective is service-first and risk-aware. Finite constructor,
  decoder, search, and charge-target budgets may miss feasible improvements.
  A direct feasible action does not prove a better end-to-end route exists.
- `K_ref` comes from an offline feasible reference, not a proven minimum
  MP fleet. `MF` or `SF` routes may be used offline because their full
  charges are allowed under MP, but no published route enters online
  observations.
- Exact-count target DoD can realize a different fraction when the
  customer count is not divisible by four. The generator rejects a target
  with insufficient eligible requests.
- Real-time latency depends on hardware and load. The five-second budget
  is measured for every decision and audited; a single noninterruptible
  operation may still create engineering overhead.
- The initial C1 pilot has one scenario per size. It cannot support
  generalization across families, DoD, seeds, or unused benchmark instances.
  Portfolio is disabled until a new selector is trained and frozen on
  Desaulniers development data.
