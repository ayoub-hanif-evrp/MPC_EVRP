# Research direction decision

**Decision (2026-11-02): B - comparative paper.** Keep the frozen five-method event-triggered MPC comparison as the primary contribution. No new MPC-ALNS method currently has both a distinct mechanism and a demonstrated, recoverable service opportunity that justifies implementation. This is a decision about present evidence, not a claim that further improvement is impossible.

The code already has six ALNS destroy operators (including time-window, battery, and route risk), four repair policies (including criticality-first and reserve-preserving), bounded ejection chains, larger recovery neighborhoods in `ALNSRecoveryV2`, selective resource-label partial charging, `service_guard_v1`, counterfactual ready first actions, and two-EV `flex_exchange` ([ALNS](../evrp/optimizers/alns.py), [targeted recovery](../evrp/targeted_recovery.py), [exchange](../evrp/flex_exchange.py)). The shared [objective](../evrp/optimizers/objective.py) already ranks uncovered customers first, then zero-/one-option customers and control/route risks; it is not a plain distance-only solver. Existing work does **not** establish that a different equal-service preference would improve final service.

## Published prior art

- Soeffker, Ulmer, and Mattfeld (2024), [*Balancing resources for dynamic vehicle routing with stochastic customer requests*](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00291-024-00747-1), study fleet-resource balance and anticipatory post-decision value approximations. They also model accepted requests as fixed commitments. This makes generic "preserve flexibility" or "respect irreversible decisions" claims weak as novelty claims.
- [A 2024 multi-depot dynamic VRPTW ALNS study](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cie.2024.110122) uses short-period reoptimization with time-window-aware removal/insertion. Reoptimization plus an urgency operator is not new by itself.
- [A 2025 electric pickup-and-delivery study](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.swevo.2025.102042) integrates adaptive neighborhood search and partial charging. Charging reconstruction or larger ALNS neighborhoods alone would not distinguish this project.
- [A 2025 multi-EV route/allocation/velocity study](https://doi.org/10.1109/TTE.2025.3592834) combines routing and MPC for energy management. Its problem differs from dynamic EVRPTW, but MPC for multi-EV operations is not novel by itself.
- [A 2025 dynamic VRPTW study](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.asej.2025.103462) compares optional and mandatory assignment while considering simulated future requests. Its assumptions differ from ours, but anticipation and customer-acceptance policies are established research directions.
- Jiang, Zhang, and Puchinger (2026), [*Dynamic Electric Vehicle Routing Problem with Stochastic Requests*](https://doi.org/10.1287/trsc.2025.0476), is directly adjacent work on stochastic requests and EV routing; the [authors' publication record](https://cv.hal.science/jakob-puchinger) confirms the journal article. A learned or stochastic lookahead extension would need a specific contribution beyond this literature, not a claim of being first to combine dynamic EV routing and lookahead.

These papers differ in constraints, objectives, and information models. Their relevance is a **novelty warning**, not evidence that their results transfer numerically to our Desaulniers experiments. The reviewed literature is not an exhaustive priority search.

## Mechanism screen

One unimplemented possibility was considered: a calibrated **post-decision continuation-value estimate** for MPC-ALNS, trained only on *past development* trajectories and evaluated from current observable fleet/time/battery features. Unlike the existing instantaneous risk and insertion-option terms, it would estimate future service after candidate dispatch actions. It could respect noninterruptible actions, partial charging, and hidden requests if the value model never receives actual unreleased customers and every proposed route still passes the original decoder and physical replay.

**Do not implement it now.** Similar anticipation/value-function ideas are published above; the repository has no validated causal training targets for counterfactual actions, no demonstrated additional-coverage witness in the two corrected transitions, and no evidence that inference plus search fits the five-second limit. Training on the completed final cohorts would contaminate evaluation. A six-to-twelve-case pilot would therefore be premature and could easily retest the same failed flexibility intuition under a new name. No candidate screened here meets all novelty, evidence, and small-pilot criteria. In particular, another customer-set guard, urgency insertion, enlarged destroy step, or charging fallback would duplicate existing code.

## Recommended next task

Prepare a **paper-only** synthesis from the frozen 450-run primary and 60-run replication: common MPC architecture, paired service and complete-service results, parent-clustered uncertainty, conditional efficiency on matched served sets, five-second timing compliance, negative development ablations, and the distinction between offline feasible references and online hidden requests. Keep cohorts separate and label six-parent/family generalization limits. No new optimizer, simulator change, result rewrite, or campaign is needed for this task.

This is feasible with the existing audited tables, traces, and figures; its smallest next deliverable is a manuscript-ready methods/results/limitations outline, not another implementation or simulation.

Stop short of an enhanced-method claim unless a later *independent*, observable-state diagnostic first demonstrates additional physically feasible coverage or a distinct decision mechanism, followed by a predeclared paired development pilot against unchanged MPC-ALNS. If a target venue requires a novel optimizer rather than a rigorous comparative systems result, select a different research question or collect genuinely new evidence; do not retroactively promote a negative variant.
