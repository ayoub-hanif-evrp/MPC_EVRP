# Paper Campaign Execution Notes

The frozen protocol is commit `8595dd6c043e35f06b350846aaf8226bdf651669`.
The controller source SHA-256 remained
`4cdead8ab9956362ba9921c0818b7bcc9690487ab11ba001d087abebe43176b6`.
All 162 predeclared holdout method runs (54 paired scenarios), 60 static
controller seeds, and 180 RoutingBlocks attempts were completed. Final raw
file paths and SHA-256 hashes are in the three campaign manifests. The
holdout manifest was generated by the original frozen runner after all
workers finished; it verified every run and scenario hash. No MPC-MCTS
algorithm or controller config was changed after the freeze.

The first 27 c109 holdout method runs and 11 c208 runs were executed
sequentially. To use the previously authorized multiple CPU cores, the
remaining holdout families ran concurrently in separate processes. Static
controller instances ran in six parallel processes; RoutingBlocks used 12
parallel independent worker processes. Thus wall times and decision latencies
from those portions reflect a loaded machine, not isolated-machine timing.
The per-decision five-second bound is still checked from each raw record;
no claim of matched total compute with RoutingBlocks is made.

RoutingBlocks 0.2.1 used the unmodified official EVRPTW-PR ALNS example and
official config, with only the instance's fleet upper bound set to the same
frozen `K_ref`. External processes used isolated CPython 3.11.15. The solver's
nominal time limit is not a hard wall-clock cap: all 60 nominal 5-second runs
exceeded it by 8.75-24.20 seconds, all 60 nominal 30-second runs exceeded it
by at most 0.46 seconds, and all 60 nominal 300-second runs exceeded it by at
most 0.38 seconds. Consequently no RoutingBlocks result qualifies as a
strictly budget-respecting comparison. Replay-valid results are shown as
offline quality context with measured runtimes, and strict-budget success is
a separate column. This timing deviation was discovered during execution;
the frozen protocol was not rewritten.

Of 180 external routes, 155 passed our independent EV physics replay; 25 did
not. Seventeen of the rejected routes were labelled feasible by RoutingBlocks.
The audit CSV retains the official flag and our rejection reason. There is no
depot-restart dynamic surrogate and no pyevrp solver result in the main table:
pyevrp's bundled loading, charging, and fleet behavior did not pass the exact
comparability gate. The earlier Rolling-LNS development records remain in the
supplementary comparison and were not overwritten.

Paper figures are PNG only. Tables, Markdown, figures, and result commentary
are regenerated from raw records with `python -m evrp.external.paper_reporting`.
The pre-holdout protocol and source hashes are unchanged by this note.
