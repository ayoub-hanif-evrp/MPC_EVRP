# Complementarity gate: not yet evaluated

The isolated development pilot contains **six** paired scenarios and 30
optimizer runs out of the planned 66 scenarios and 330 runs. Its trajectory
results are preliminary; they do not establish a common-state
service-complementarity gap. The earlier four-method pilot was non-isolated
and is preserved only as historical development evidence.

Trajectory-level outcomes are not the common-state shadow outcomes required
for SBS/VBS. No snapshot dataset, SBS/VBS estimate, grouped selector training,
portfolio claim or new final holdout exists. A one-scenario snapshot capture
smoke test is not a frozen shadow dataset. The remaining development grid
and shadow-snapshot study are required before the scientific stop rule can be
decided.
