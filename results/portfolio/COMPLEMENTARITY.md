# Complementarity gate: not yet evaluated

The current-source development manifest contains **six** paired scenarios and
24 optimizer runs out of the planned 66 scenarios and 264 runs. The audited
[six-family pilot](SIX_FAMILY_PILOT.md) reports trajectory-level service
wins/ties/losses. ALNS was never worse in service in that pilot, so it does
not yet demonstrate a meaningful service-complementarity gap.

Trajectory-level outcomes are not the common-state shadow outcomes required
for SBS/VBS. No snapshot dataset, SBS/VBS estimate, grouped selector training,
portfolio claim or new final holdout exists. The 60 remaining development
scenarios and shadow-snapshot study are required before the scientific stop
rule can be decided.
