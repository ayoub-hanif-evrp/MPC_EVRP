# Charging-decoder insertion probe

Development ALNS losses only. Each probe uses the first visible decision, measured ready states, observable customers, and the selected soft customer orders. Insertion is tested without changing the other route orders. Each decoder gets at most four seconds per lost customer; timeouts are inconclusive.
