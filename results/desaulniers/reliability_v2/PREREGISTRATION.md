# Reliability V2: prospective research protocol

## Status and historical boundary

This protocol is written before any new closed-loop comparison outcomes.
Starting main: 15aaa652be6b055389606139007c1f6509e0ff7d. The original final,
robustness and targeted-service studies remain immutable and separately cited.
The 168 offline references are already independently validated; no new offline

## Evidence and one new hypothesis

Read-only analysis of 72 original targeted development trajectories identifies
128 eventual misses: 15 already fail the optimistic direct-time test at release,
and 113 later fail it. Of the latter, 101 first fail immediately after measured
dispatches. Idle waits are interruptible; serving, charging and return actions
remain immutable. This is conditional state evidence, not proof dispatch caused
the final miss or that a globally feasible replacement exists. Positive counts
ignore battery, load and other obligations and are not insertion witnesses.
All reconstructed roots, historical raw digests and event sequences are saved.

The distinct new hypothesis is that a simultaneous two-EV head exchange, or an
uncovered urgent request plus relocation of the displaced head, can expose
service-preserving controls not generated by the previous single-head moves.
Generation uses current direct-arrival slack and only released work. Canonical
scoring, including original constructor ownership anchoring, is unchanged.
The old 20 selected diagnostic roots yield no improvement even with this new
neighborhood: this negative evidence is retained, not hidden. These roots do
not exhaust earlier dispatches or independent parent cases.

## Implementations and ablations

- `original`: all five original engines and physical/event-trigger logic.
- `flex_exchange`: at most 48 generated exchanges and six selective resource-label
  attempts after original reconstruction fails. Reserve 0.4 s from the optimizer;
  the bounded control probe receives at most 0.35 s within the original 5 s.
  Keep every validated strict canonical improvement if the probe times out.
  Original search may still make equal-coverage swaps; it is not constrained by
  this additional service-preserving neighborhood. No extra waiting, reserves,
  future-demand model or new event trigger is introduced.
- `cache_only`: decision-local exact replay memo, capped at 4096 entries, with
  state, actions, complete observation and MPC config keys. Deadline checks
  precede hits. Root caches are reset on normal completion and exceptions.
  The cache is also enabled in `flex_exchange`; cache-only comparison measures
  this component separately rather than attributing its effects to exchanges.
- `completion_first_action`: the unchanged prior V1 first-action probe, tested
  prospectively for Completion only. This is a distinct predeclared hypothesis,
  not a retroactive change to the previous ALNS-centered promotion decision.

All routes face independent physical replay. No optimizer core, mathematical
objective, normalization, MP physics, fleet or request release rule is altered.
Raw latency is shared-machine end-to-end decision time, not isolated certification.

## Independence, design and compute bound

Twelve new development parents, two per family, are specified in
`configs/reliability_v2.yaml`. Following the preserved no-outcome structural
preflight, select the first two lexicographic unexposed, nonreserved parents
per family for which all three sizes have audited eligibility at DoD .75.
This is selection on reference-preserving generator eligibility, not online
performance. It restricts generalization to high-eligibility benchmark cases;
the excluded low-eligibility cases are not claimed as evaluated. See
preflight/AMENDMENT.md. They are checked against prior online manifests
before outcomes open. Every parent is evaluated at 100 customers and one smaller
size (25/50 alternated within each family). This is a balanced partial size panel,
not a complete parent-size factorial. Size-specific inference does not pretend
that related variants are independent. The design has 12 independent parents.

For each selected file, fully cross DoD .25/.75 with seeds 0/1. This removes the
previous within-file seed/DoD confounding. Planned design: 96 scenarios, 960
original/flex runs across all five engines, 96 additional Completion V1 runs,
and 60 cache-only ablation runs (100 customers, .75 DoD, seed0, all 12 parents
and all five engines): 1116 runs before any structural eligibility exclusions.
Exclude only generator-ineligible DoD cases, before online outcomes, equally
for every paired method. Record the exact eligible denominator in the protocol.

Use the existing full engine parameters and sequential study workers. Other
workloads are left running. A whole-worker watchdog of 3900 s preserves failures.
Each invocation permits 12 hours and pauses only at a completed scenario boundary.
No unfavorable raw is removed or silently rerun. Source, data, config and exact
archived reference/scenario bytes are hashed before the first worker; resumed
valid raws are reused byte-for-byte. No tuning follows interim results.

## Promotion and uncertainty

Resample whole parents (all sizes, DoD and seeds together), 20000 draws with
seed20261024. Two primary hypotheses use Bonferroni 97.5% two-sided parent
bootstrap intervals. These are finite-cluster exploratory development evidence,
not exact finite-sample familywise guarantees or independent final confirmation.

1. ALNS + flex: lower interval for 100-customer complete-service change >0;
   lower overall service interval >=0; nonnegative total and 100-customer
   service gain; nonnegative overall complete gain and service gain per seed;
   all 12 parents represented; every audit and 5-second check passes.
2. Completion + V1 first action: the same safeguards, but the lower service
   interval must be strictly positive. This gate is prospectively Completion-
   specific; it does not overturn the frozen previous ALNS decision.

Other engines, cache ablations and strata are reported descriptively. If both
primary hypotheses pass, fixed priority selects flex. Do not invent a further
candidate if neither passes; retain the original system and report a completed
negative development result. Defaults remain original throughout development.

## Conditional independent confirmation

Reserve c102_21, c202_21, r102_21, r202_21, rc102_21, rc202_21, including every
size variant. These were reserved before the prior study, which opened no
holdout. Recheck exposure before freezing their new online evaluation. Use the
same existing 168-reference inventory and matched inputs for originals and the
selected variant; historical fleets and releases are never replaced.

Only after audited development promotion, cross all three sizes, both DoD and
both seeds on these six parents. Flex is compared with all five original engines;
a Completion-only selection compares V1 with Completion and includes the other
four unchanged engines as context, without claiming their enhanced performance.
The independent confirmation must again support a positive 100-customer complete
interval, no negative service interval or aggregate complete/service loss.
No selector, threshold or algorithm is revised using those outcomes.

## Reporting

Report service, complete service, 100-customer outcomes, every paired gain and
regression, parent intervals, seed/DoD/parent strata, constructor/optimizer
deadlines, end-to-end latency, cumulative compute, memo hits/misses and accepted
exchange candidates. Distance/EV comparisons condition on identical served
customer sets. Tables are Markdown; figures PNG at nominal300dpi only. JSON
raws and manifests remain for reproducibility. `FINAL_REPORT.md` always states
whether results are partial development, completed development or independent
final evidence; no partial or failed gate becomes a superiority claim.
